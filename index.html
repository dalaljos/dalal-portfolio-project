About Me

I'm an entrepreneur and student pursuing a degree in Information Science and Technologies at Michigan State University. Since August 2022, I have served as the CEO and founder of my own company. I use analytical thinking and data-based strategies to develop investment solutions for my clients while building trust and long-term relationships.

I also recently founded a private firm focused on stock trading and financial strategy. I oversee trade execution, market analysis, and the development of proprietary strategies based on intraday liquidity, volume, and macroeconomic trends. This work reflects my interest in precision, risk management, and finding new ways to understand the markets.

Outside of business, I serve as Vice President and Founder of Spartans for Duggan. I help lead campaign strategy, manage a team of interns, and create youth-focused political content supporting Mike Duggan's run for governor. This role gives me an opportunity to connect leadership and communication with my interest in social impact.

As a client of Tazame, I stay connected with expert mentors and business leaders who help me grow my ventures with a clear sense of direction. Across business, technology, and community involvement, my goal is to create systems and strategies that provide real value and measurable change.
